A little about my journey

My story: I know what it feels like to start with very little.

When I started exploring online business, I was a student with big hopes but very limited resources. Even buying internet data was sometimes a challenge.

There were days I worked on the farm, weeding from morning until evening. I had to find ways to manage basic needs while still trying to learn a skill that could create another opportunity for me.

I borrowed money at a point to sort out food, data and the training I needed to learn affiliate marketing. It was not a smooth journey, and I had to make sacrifices to keep learning.

My first major milestone was earning GH₵2,000 online.

That experience changed how I looked at the internet. I began to understand that a smartphone could be more than a device for entertainment when you learn how to use it to solve problems and connect products with people who need them.

Within my first three months in online marketing, I generated over $800. Since then, I have continued learning, practising and helping other beginners understand the process.

I share this because I know what it feels like to want to do something for yourself but not know where to begin. My aim is to make the learning process clearer and more practical for people starting out.
